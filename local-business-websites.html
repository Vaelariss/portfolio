A local business website has one job, and it is not winning design awards. Someone has found you, they are deciding whether to trust you, and they need your prices, your hours and a way to contact you without hunting. Most small-business sites bury all three.

I build the opposite: the things people actually came for are in the first screen, the contact route is always one tap away, and the whole thing loads fast on a phone with two bars of signal — which is where most of your visitors really are.

What you get

Everything included, nothing billed later.

  • Custom design, not a template

    Built around your trade and your brand. It will not look like the other seven salons in your town.

  • Click-to-call and directions

    Tap-to-ring, WhatsApp, and a map that opens the customer's own maps app. No copying numbers by hand.

  • Built to be found

    Proper titles, descriptions, structured data and a sitemap so search engines can actually read what you do and where.

  • Fast on mobile

    Most local searches happen on a phone, often on mobile data. Speed is a feature, not a nice-to-have.

  • Booking links wired in

    Connects to whatever you already use — Booksy, Fresha, Calendly, Treatwell — rather than forcing you to change systems.

  • No monthly fees

    One payment, the site is yours. Hosting is on you and typically costs nothing at this size. Optional care plan if you want me to keep it current.
