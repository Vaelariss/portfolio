The deal

50% the first time. 10% every time after.

The 50% resets for each service I offer, not once for you overall. So the first website you introduce pays 50%. The second website pays 10%. But the first AI receptionist you introduce is a new service, so that one pays 50% again — then 10% for receptionists after that.

Five services, five chances at a first sale. That is deliberate: the point is to reward you for opening a door I have never walked through, and there is a different door for each thing I build.

One exception, and I would rather be straight about why. Custom-built systems pay a flat 10% — first sale and every one after. A website is a few days’ work; a bespoke system is six to eight weeks of it. Half the fee on a build that size would mean earning less per hour than I do on anything else I sell, and an arrangement that only works while nobody uses it is not an arrangement. Ten percent of a custom build is £395 to £645, which is a bigger cheque than a first website introduction pays.

Worked example

Say you introduce a barber who buys a £895 website. That is your first website sale, so you get £447.50.

Two months later you introduce a café who also buys a website. Second website sale — you get £89.50.

Then you introduce a dentist who buys an AI receptionist. Different service, first one you have sent me — back to 50%, so £375 at the founding rate.

How it works

Four things worth knowing before you start.

  • You get a link, not a login

    Your link is just the site with your code on the end — brickworkstudio.net/?ref=yourname. Anyone who arrives through it is tagged to you, and the tag follows them around the site and onto the enquiry form. Message me and I will set your code up.

  • You have 90 days

    The tag lasts 90 days from the click. Long enough for someone to think it over, short enough that nobody claims a customer who turned up a year later off a Google search.

  • First introduction wins

    If someone arrives through your link and later clicks a different partner's link, the credit stays with you. Whoever made the introduction gets paid for it.

  • A word to me counts too

    Links are the tidy version, not the only version. If someone rings or WhatsApps me and says your name, tell me beforehand and it counts the same. I would rather pay you than win on a technicality.

Getting paid

When the money actually moves.

Commission is paid once the client's final balance has cleared, not when they pay the deposit. I work on a 50/50 split, so paying out on the deposit would mean paying you for a job that could still be refunded or abandoned. Expect it within 14 days of that final payment, by bank transfer.

If a client refunds or cancels after commission has been paid, it comes off your next one rather than being invoiced back to you.

You are responsible for declaring this as income wherever you live. I do not withhold tax and I am not able to advise you on it.

The rules

Short list, and I mean all of it.

  • Say that you earn from it

    If you recommend me publicly — a post, a video, a group, a story — you have to say you get paid if they buy. In the US that is not a courtesy, it is the FTC's endorsement rules, and it lands on me as well as you. “I get a commission if you use them” is enough.

  • Do not promise on my behalf

    Do not invent prices, timelines or results. If you are unsure what I can do, ask — a client arriving with the wrong expectation costs everyone more than the introduction was worth.

  • No self-referral

    Your own business, your own accounts, or a company you are part of do not count. If you want a site for yourself, just ask me for a price.

  • No spam, no paid ads on my name

    No unsolicited bulk email or DMs, and no buying ads against “Brickwork Studio” or my domain. That traffic was already coming to me.

Break these and the arrangement ends, and anything unpaid stays unpaid. I would rather never have to do that. This page is the whole agreement, it is not a job or a partnership in the legal sense, and I may change the rates for future introductions — never for one already made.
