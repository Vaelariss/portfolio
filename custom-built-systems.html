There is a particular kind of waste that every growing business hits: three tools that each do 70% of the job, a spreadsheet holding them together, and someone re-typing the same information into all four. It works, in the sense that nobody has died. It also quietly eats hours every week.

A custom system replaces that with one thing that does the whole job. We scope it for free, give you a fixed quote before you commit a penny, and you own the finished system outright.

What you get

Everything included, nothing billed later.

  • Free scoping first

    We map what you do now, where the time goes and what a fix is actually worth. If the honest answer is that existing software is good enough, we say so.

  • Fixed quote before you commit

    You see the full price before any money changes hands. No hourly billing, no scope creep invoices, no surprise final bill.

  • Built around your process

    Your terminology, your steps, your edge cases — not a generic template you have to bend yourself around.

  • Connects what you already use

    Plugs into your existing calendar, payments, email and spreadsheets rather than demanding you abandon them.

  • You own it outright

    The finished system and its code are yours. No per-seat licence, no ransom when you want to leave.

  • Documented in plain English

    Written so whoever comes after you can understand it, including you in six months.
