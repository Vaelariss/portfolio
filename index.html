The maths

Work out what it would take to pay for itself.

I'm not going to tell you what a website will earn you — I don't know your business and anyone who claims otherwise is guessing. So do it the other way round: put in your own numbers and see how much would have to change before the build breaks even.

£
%

How that's worked out: build price ÷ (average job × your margin) = jobs needed. Nothing else is assumed, and the answer is only as good as the two numbers you typed.

All this does is divide one number by another. It tells you what would have to happen for the build to pay for itself, and nothing at all about whether it will. A website can't make anyone call you, and I don't promise rankings, traffic, enquiries or sales. If the number above looks like a stretch for your business, don't buy the site. I'd rather tell you that now than after you've paid.
