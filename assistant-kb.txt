Brickwork Studio is a one-person web design studio at brickworkstudio.net. Remote from Wolverhampton, UK, working with clients in the US and UK, no
agency, one project at a time. Prices are fixed and published, not quote-on-request. Work starts
with a free visual mockup, so the client sees the design before paying anything.

## Process

- Free mockup: a real design around the client's brand, not a template, within 48 hours of the
  call. One per business. If they don't like it they owe nothing.
- Build: 7 working days, counted from the day the client sends their content — not from first
  contact.
- Revisions: two rounds during the build, then a 14-day snag list after launch.
- The client provides: business name, what they do, any brand colours or logos, a couple of sites
  they like. About ten minutes. Placeholder photography is used until they have their own.
- Existing sites: the studio will say honestly what is holding one back, including when a patch
  beats a rebuild.
